# Benchmark scope: fiscal compliance and digital accounting products

**Classification:** `CANONICAL_ACTIVE`  
**Status:** `DISCOVERY_BASELINE`

## Purpose

Benchmark publicly observable capabilities and authorized trial behavior without copying protected
implementation details.

## Capability families

### Compliance-oriented

- portfolio and legal-entity views;
- fiscal status and certificate monitoring;
- obligation calendar;
- e-CAC messages;
- guides and declarations;
- alerts, historical evidence and operational dashboards;
- health/risk score and diagnostics;
- grounded explanatory assistance.

### Digital-accounting-oriented

- operational routines;
- invoice workflows;
- taxes and payment guides;
- bank-statement import/classification;
- document requests and human-assisted workflows;
- payroll/pro-labore support;
- accounting and financial reports.

## Evidence levels

- `PUBLIC_MARKETING` — capability claim only;
- `PUBLIC_DOCUMENTATION` — documented public workflow;
- `AUTHORIZED_TRIAL_OBSERVATION` — behavior observed in an authorized trial;
- `INTERNAL_REQUIREMENT` — approved target-company need;
- `IMPLEMENTATION_EVIDENCE` — repository/runtime proof.

Only `INTERNAL_REQUIREMENT` authorizes product scope. Benchmark observations never authorize direct
copying of content, design, rules or algorithms.
