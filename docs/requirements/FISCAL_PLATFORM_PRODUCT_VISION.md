# Fiscal Operations Platform product vision

**Classification:** `CANONICAL_ACTIVE`  
**Status:** `WORKING_BASELINE`

## Purpose

Create an internal operational center for one company's fiscal and accounting activities. The
platform consolidates companies and establishments, recurring obligations, certificates, messages,
guides, documents, assignments, evidence, integrations, alerts and management indicators.

## Principles

- Internal product, not a public SaaS.
- Incremental implementation by approved phases.
- Official APIs before portal automation.
- Manual/assisted workflows remain valid where no safe API exists.
- Backend-authoritative rules and states.
- Evidence and audit before automation.
- PostgreSQL remains the source of truth.
- External-source failure is explicit and never converted to false regularity.
- AI explains grounded facts; it does not create authoritative fiscal conclusions.
- Public benchmarks inform discovery only.

## Initial phases

1. Technical foundation and clean product baseline.
2. Organization, company, establishment and tax-registration model.
3. Operational routines, obligations, occurrences, tasks and documents.
4. Certificates, fiscal queries, messages, guides and compliance findings.
5. Official connectors and assisted-source workflows.
6. Global search, reporting and technical administration.
7. Internal risk score, diagnostics, BI and grounded AI assistance.
